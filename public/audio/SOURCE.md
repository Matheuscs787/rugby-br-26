# Referee whistle

`referee-whistle.mp3` is adapted from “Referee whistle blow, gymnasium.wav” by
SpliceSound:

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/SpliceSound/sounds/218318/
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source was converted to mono at 32 kHz, trimmed to the first whistle blow,
faded at the boundaries and encoded as a 64 kbps MP3.
